# Redmi 11T Pro: artifact publication boundary

[中文](README_zh-CN.md) | English

The source investigation used MediaTek TA, uTsecManager/boot-chain modifications, stock firmware, module readbacks, and raw Keybox output. These may contain vendor-copyrighted material, unique device information, or reusable secrets. The current `main` branch distributes no original files, compressed copies, patch bytes, or executable extraction scripts.

The public [tutorial](../01_教程/Tutorial_en.md), [practice record](../02_流水记录/Practice_Log_en.md), and [principles](../03_原理/Principles_en.md) retain the research target, mechanism, failures, and acceptance relationships. Third-party firmware or tools must come from channels authorized by their rights holders under the applicable terms. A filename, hash, or research-only label is not redistribution permission.

Private research originals remain local and are not in the current `main` branch's commit history. Any future public artifact requires a separate review of rights, secrets, and device identifiers.
